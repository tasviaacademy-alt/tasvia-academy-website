Parent reminders

A simple checklist to help students stay organised and prepared.

DAILY

Learning routine

  • Help your child follow the assigned study and revision routine.
  • Check that books, notebooks and stationery are ready.
  • Encourage reading and independent practice.
WEEKLY

Review progress

  • Review completed homework and classwork.
  • Discuss difficult topics and doubts with your child.
  • Check academy messages for updates from staff.
EXAM TIME

Be prepared

  • Confirm examination dates and reporting instructions from official notices.
  • Plan revision in manageable sessions.
  • Ensure adequate rest before the examination.
