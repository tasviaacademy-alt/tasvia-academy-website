Practice corner

Select a subject below. These are sample practice questions, not official previous-year examination questions.

1. Find the next number: 2, 4, 8, 16, __.
Answer: 32. Each number is multiplied by 2.
2. Which one is different from the others?
Answer: Circle. It has no straight sides or corners.
3. If CAT is coded as DBU (each letter moves one step forward), DOG is coded as:
Answer: EPH. D→E, O→P and G→H.
1. What is 25% of 200?
Answer: 50. One-fourth of 200 is 50.
2. A notebook costs ₹18. What is the cost of 5 notebooks?
Answer: ₹90. 18 × 5 = 90.
3. Find the perimeter of a square with side 7 cm.
Answer: 28 cm. Perimeter = 4 × side = 4 × 7.
1. What is the capital of Telangana?
Answer: Hyderabad.
2. Which planet is known as the Red Planet?
Answer: Mars.
3. What is the national animal of India?
Answer: Bengal Tiger.
4. How many days are there in a leap year?
Answer: 366 days.
5. Which is the largest ocean on Earth?
Answer: Pacific Ocean.
1. Choose the correctly spelt word.
Answer: Beautiful.
2. Choose the opposite of “Ancient”.
Answer: Modern.
3. Fill in the blank: She ___ to school every day.
Answer: goes.
