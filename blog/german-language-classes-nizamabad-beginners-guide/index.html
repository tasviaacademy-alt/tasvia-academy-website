German Language Classes in Nizamabad: A Beginner’s Guide

Introducing a new language can help children explore sounds, words and cultures. Here is what parents should know about the beginner German learning programme being introduced at TASVIA Academy.

By TASVIA Academy · 12 November 2026 · 4 min read
At a glance: TASVIA Academy is introducing beginner German language learning for students from LKG to Intermediate. No previous German knowledge is required to enquire about the introductory programme.

Why introduce children to another language?

Learning a new language gives students an opportunity to notice different sounds, practise new vocabulary and communicate in another way. It can also encourage curiosity about other cultures. The right approach should be enjoyable, age-appropriate and paced according to each learner.

German learning at TASVIA Academy

The German programme is being introduced as an additional learning opportunity alongside regular tuition. Beginner activities are planned according to the student’s class and learning stage.

LKG to Class 2

Play-based exposure to greetings, colours, numbers, simple words, songs and picture activities.

Classes 3 to 5

Everyday vocabulary, simple phrases, pronunciation and short guided conversations.

Classes 6 to 10

Beginner grammar, sentence building, reading, listening and guided speaking practice.

Intermediate

Structured beginner learning with practical communication and vocabulary development.

What can beginners learn?

  • German alphabet and pronunciation.
  • Greetings and introductions.
  • Numbers, colours, days and everyday vocabulary.
  • Basic sentence formation and beginner grammar.
  • Listening, speaking, reading and writing practice.

How can parents support language learning?

Encourage short, regular practice rather than memorising long lists. Ask your child to use a greeting, repeat a new word or share a phrase learned in class. Positive encouragement and consistent revision can help build confidence.
