1. Tuition for LKG to Intermediate

TASVIA Academy offers tuition support for learners from LKG to Intermediate, covering all subjects. Students can strengthen concepts, practise school lessons, complete homework with guidance and prepare for academic assessments.

  • LKG and UKG: early learning, reading and writing readiness, number recognition and foundational skills.
  • Primary classes: subject learning, basic concepts, reading, writing, numeracy and homework support.
  • Classes 6 to 10: subject-wise explanations, practice, revision and school-test preparation.
  • Intermediate: subject support, doubt clarification and structured revision.

Parents can discuss the child’s class, school syllabus, subjects and learning needs with the academy before choosing a suitable arrangement.

An Introduction to German Language Learning

Alongside regular academic tuition, TASVIA Academy is introducing German language learning as an additional learning opportunity for students from LKG to Intermediate. Learning a new language can help children become familiar with different sounds, words and ways of communicating while building curiosity about other cultures.

Beginners can start with simple greetings, the German alphabet and pronunciation, everyday vocabulary and basic phrases. Learning activities can be adapted to the learner’s age and level, with an emphasis on listening, speaking and gradual confidence-building.

Interested in learning German?
Explore the TASVIA German Language programme or contact the academy to ask about the introductory batches, timetable and fees. Class schedules and batch availability will be confirmed by the academy.

3. Abacus and entrance coaching

Alongside regular tuition, TASVIA Academy offers Abacus training to support number sense and calculation practice. Enquire about the suitable level and current training schedule.

The academy also provides focused coaching for Navodaya (JNVST) and Sainik School (AISSEE) entrance examinations. These are separate examinations with different eligibility rules and preparation requirements. Ask the team about the target class, syllabus coverage, practice plan and current batch schedule.
