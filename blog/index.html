SCHOOL DIRECTORY · INDIA

Navodaya & Sainik Schools Directory

Explore Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas and Sainik Schools in Telangana and across India. Check the official school website or admission notification before applying.

Important: “Functional” and “sanctioned” are different. The Telangana Navodaya list below follows the Government of India Lok Sabha reply dated 2 February 2026. Newly approved partnership Sainik Schools are listed separately by the Sainik Schools Society.

2. Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas – All India

The national directory covers JNVs across States and Union Territories. Use the official NVS portal for the latest school-wise list, regional details, admission notices and eligibility rules. A Government of India parliamentary reply in April 2025 reported 689 sanctioned JNVs nationwide; sanctioned numbers and operational status may change.
