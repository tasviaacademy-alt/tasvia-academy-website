Navodaya & Sainik School Entrance Coaching in Nizamabad: A Parent’s Guide

Two respected school pathways, two different entrance examinations. Here is a clear guide to help families understand the exams and build a steady preparation plan for their child.

By TASVIA Academy  ·  Updated 30 October 2026  ·  7 min read
Start with the right exam: Navodaya admission to Class VI is through JNVST. Sainik School admission to Class VI and Class IX is through AISSEE. Their eligibility rules, syllabus and selection process are different, so prepare using the correct official notification.

1. Know the difference: JNVST and AISSEE

JNVST (Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test) is the entrance test for admission to Class VI in Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas. The test generally assesses mental ability, arithmetic and language skills. The district of the school in which the child studies Class V is important for determining the applicable JNV and eligibility. Parents should carefully check the current prospectus and district-wise instructions.

AISSEE (All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination) is conducted for admission to Class VI and Class IX in Sainik Schools. The paper and subject coverage vary by entry class. Mathematics, language, intelligence and general academic knowledge are important preparation areas; Class IX aspirants should also follow the prescribed Science and Social Science syllabus in the current bulletin.

These examinations are not interchangeable. Before choosing a coaching plan, confirm your child’s target class, age eligibility, school records and the latest official application requirements.

Parent reminder: Exam dates, age limits, reservation rules, district mapping, seat availability and syllabus can change from year to year. Refer to the official JNVST/NVS and AISSEE/NTA notifications for the admission year you are applying for.

2. What should students prepare?

For Navodaya Class VI (JNVST)

  • Mental Ability: patterns, figure completion, analogy, classification, series and spatial reasoning.
  • Arithmetic: number operations, fractions, decimals, measurement, time, money and practical word problems as prescribed in the current syllabus.
  • Language: reading comprehension, vocabulary and understanding passages.

For Sainik School Class VI (AISSEE)

  • Mathematics: strengthen concepts, calculation accuracy and multi-step problem solving.
  • Language: grammar, vocabulary and reading comprehension according to the prescribed paper.
  • Intelligence: logical reasoning, patterns, series and analytical thinking.
  • General Knowledge: age-appropriate awareness of science, society, environment and current topics within the syllabus.

For Sainik School Class IX (AISSEE)

Class IX aspirants need a broader subject plan. Along with Mathematics, English and Intelligence, students should prepare Science and Social Science topics according to the latest official syllabus. Do not rely on a Class VI study plan for Class IX.

3. Build a realistic study routine

Consistency is more useful than irregular long study sessions. Start with a timetable that fits the child’s school hours, rest and other responsibilities.

  1. Concept learning: understand one topic at a time before moving to difficult questions.
  2. Daily practice: solve a short set of questions from the topics already taught.
  3. Reasoning practice: include a small, regular reasoning session to build speed and confidence.
  4. Revision: revisit previously learned topics every week.
  5. Test and review: attempt timed tests and discuss mistakes rather than only checking the score.

4. Use practice tests effectively

Mock tests help students become familiar with question styles, timing and OMR answer marking where applicable. A useful test routine includes:

  • Practise with questions aligned to the correct exam and class.
  • Use a timer and follow the test instructions.
  • Review incorrect and unanswered questions after each test.
  • Maintain a simple record of topics that need more practice.
  • Repeat weak topics before attempting the next full-length test.

A mock-test score is a learning indicator, not a guarantee of admission. The goal is to identify gaps early and improve steadily.

5. How parents can support preparation

  • Keep a regular study and sleep routine without overloading the child.
  • Encourage questions and explain that mistakes are part of learning.
  • Check whether homework, revision and practice are being completed.
  • Discuss test performance calmly and focus on improvement areas.
  • Keep application documents and school records ready, and verify details before submission.

7. Frequently asked questions

Can a student prepare for both Navodaya and Sainik School?

Yes, a student may explore both pathways if they meet each examination’s eligibility requirements. Since the papers and rules differ, preparation should include exam-specific practice.

Which class should a child be in to prepare for Navodaya Class VI?

Students generally prepare while studying Class V, but admission eligibility depends on the current prospectus, including age and schooling conditions. Always verify the official rules for the relevant year.

Does AISSEE have the same syllabus for Class VI and Class IX?

No. The subjects and syllabus differ by entry class. Use the current NTA information bulletin for the exact syllabus and examination pattern.
