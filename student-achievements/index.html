Ask About Student Support

How we support progress

A structured approach helps students understand what they have learned, where they need support and what to work on next.

01

Learning goals

Identify the student's class, subject requirements and learning objectives.

02

Practice & revision

Build understanding through guided exercises, revision and regular practice.

03

Assessment & feedback

Use practice tests and feedback to recognise strengths and areas needing attention.

04

Focused improvement

Plan the next learning steps based on observed performance and individual needs.

05

Parent communication

Encourage constructive communication between parents, students and the academy.

06

Celebrate effort

Recognise consistency, improvement, participation and responsible learning habits.
