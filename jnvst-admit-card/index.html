JNVST Class 6 Admit Card – Download Help

Parents can download the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test hall ticket from the official NVS portal using the student's registration details. Follow these steps or contact TASVIA Academy for guidance.

Official NVS portal

How to download the hall ticket

  1. Open the official NVS candidate portal using the button below.
  2. Find the Class VI JNVST 2027 admit card link under the candidate/admit card section.
  3. Enter the student's registration number and date of birth as requested on the portal.
  4. Submit the details and check the student's name, photograph, exam date, reporting time and examination centre.
  5. Download the hall ticket and take a clear printed copy for the examination.
Open Official NVS Portal ↗ Get Help on WhatsApp
Safety: Enter registration details only on the official NVS portal. Never share OTPs, passwords or sensitive login information.
