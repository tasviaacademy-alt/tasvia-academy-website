Academic Calendar
2026–27

A month-by-month learning guide for TASVIA Academy students and parents, covering school learning, revision, practice assessments and entrance-exam preparation.

Individual school schedules, academy batch tests, class timings and holidays may differ. TASVIA will confirm its own test dates and special notices directly with parents. Always follow the latest official school/board notification.

Monthly learning plan

Suggested focus for the remaining months of the 2026–27 academic session. Teachers may adjust the plan according to class level, syllabus and student progress.

November 2026

BUILD & REINFORCE
  • Review current class topics and identify learning gaps.
  • Strengthen mathematics and language fundamentals.
  • Continue JNVST and AISSEE topic-wise practice.
  • Set individual revision targets.

December 2026

PRACTICE & REVIEW
  • Continue syllabus-based learning and written practice.
  • Use short topic tests to check understanding.
  • Practise reasoning, arithmetic and reading skills.
  • Review progress with parents where appropriate.

January 2027

REVISION & EXAM READINESS
  • Revise completed chapters and key concepts.
  • Practise timed question sets and mock exercises.
  • Work on accuracy, time management and exam habits.
  • Check official entrance-exam notices and instructions.

February 2027

CONSOLIDATE LEARNING
  • Continue class-wise revision and doubt clarification.
  • Complete practice papers appropriate to the student's level.
  • Strengthen weaker topics through targeted exercises.
  • Prepare for school and board assessments.

March 2027

ASSESS & IMPROVE
  • Follow the student's school examination schedule.
  • Use revision plans and practice tests where suitable.
  • Review mistakes and reinforce essential concepts.
  • Maintain a balanced study and rest routine.

April 2027

REVIEW & NEXT STEPS
  • Review learning outcomes and progress from the year.
  • Complete pending revision and assessments as scheduled.
  • Discuss next-class learning goals with parents.
  • Share academy holiday or new-batch updates when confirmed.

Monthly parent checklist

At the start of each month

  • Check academy and school notices.
  • Confirm any test dates or schedule changes.
  • Set realistic learning goals with your child.

During the month

  • Encourage regular homework and revision.
  • Check parent portal updates and messages.
  • Contact the academy if your child needs support.

At month end

  • Review completed topics and practice work.
  • Recognise effort and improvement.
  • Plan the next month's revision priorities.
