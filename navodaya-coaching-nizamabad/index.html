JNVST 2027 EVS Preparation – Complete Focus Areas

For the Class VI JNVST 2027, the first test section combines 20 Mental Ability questions (25 marks) and 20 Environmental Studies (EVS) questions (25 marks). EVS is therefore an important part of the entrance preparation and should be practised systematically—not treated as an optional extra.

EVS topics students should prepare

  • Natural world: plants, animals, habitats, food chains, environment, weather, water and natural resources.
  • Human body: sense organs, body systems at a basic level, health, hygiene, nutrition and common health practices.
  • Science in daily life: materials and their uses, everyday scientific observations, simple machines, energy, safety and practical problem-solving.
  • Social surroundings: family and community, occupations, transport, communication, public places, local environment and responsible citizenship.

EVS question practice at TASVIA

  • Topic-wise objective questions and concept checks.
  • Observation-based and real-life application questions.
  • Short passage followed by comprehension and EVS questions.
  • Timed practice, error analysis and regular revision.
JNVST 2027 pattern: 20 EVS questions carry 25 marks. Mental Ability and EVS are combined in the first 60-minute section. The minimum qualifying requirement is stated for the combined Mental Ability + EVS section—not as a separate EVS-only pass mark. Arithmetic and Language each have 20 questions for 25 marks. The complete test has 80 questions, 100 marks and a duration of 2 hours.
Important for parents: Older JNVST guides may show the previous pattern with 40 Mental Ability questions and no separate EVS block. For the 2027 admission cycle, follow the applicable NVS prospectus and check the official notification for any updates.

JNVST 2027 Eligibility — Important for Parents

District rule: For the 2027 Class VI admission cycle, the child should apply for the JNV associated with the district of residence under the current NVS rules. A child studying in a neighbouring district may fall under the prescribed 10 km exception, subject to verification. Wrong-district applications can be rejected.
Class VI snapshot: Candidates should be studying in Class V during 2026–27, meet the current date-of-birth and schooling requirements, and should not have already passed or repeated Class V. The JNVST selection test can be attempted only once.
