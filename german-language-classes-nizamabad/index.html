German learning for LKG to Intermediate

Every age group learns differently. Our programme is planned to introduce German in a way that matches the learner's age, school level and pace. Beginners are welcome; no previous German knowledge is required.

🌈

LKG to Class 2

Play-based introduction through greetings, colours, numbers, simple words, songs and picture activities.

📚

Classes 3 to 5

Everyday vocabulary, basic phrases, pronunciation, simple questions and short conversations.

🗣️

Classes 6 to 10

Beginner grammar, sentence formation, reading, listening and guided speaking practice.

🎓

Intermediate

Structured beginner-level learning with practical communication, vocabulary building and foundation for further study.

What students will learn

🔤 Alphabet & Pronunciation

Recognise German letters and practise clear, correct sounds.

💬 Everyday Vocabulary

Greetings, introductions, family, school, numbers, days, colours and common expressions.

✍️ Basic Grammar

Build simple words and sentences, with grammar introduced step by step according to age.

🎧 Four Language Skills

Develop listening, speaking, reading and writing through guided activities and practice.

How learning is supported

  • Beginner-friendly lessons with gradual progression.
  • Games, flashcards, worksheets and speaking activities suited to the age group.
  • Regular practice and simple progress checks.
  • Learning materials and homework guidance where appropriate.
  • Parent updates through the TASVIA parent communication channel, as the programme is introduced.

Course level, batch formation, timetable and fees will be shared by the academy during enquiry. Course completion does not automatically represent an externally accredited language certificate.
