Questions

Helpful answers about TASVIA Academy programmes and the admission enquiry process.

Which classes do you support?

Regular tuition is offered from LKG to Intermediate. Please contact the academy to confirm the subjects and current batch availability for your class.

Do you offer Navodaya coaching?

Yes. TASVIA offers preparation for the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST).

Do you offer Sainik School coaching?

Yes. TASVIA offers preparation for the All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination (AISSEE).

Is Abacus training available?

Yes. Foundation and Abacus learning is among the programmes offered at TASVIA Academy.

Do you offer German language classes?

Yes. TASVIA offers beginner-friendly German language learning for students from LKG to Intermediate. Contact the academy for current batch availability, timetable and fees.

Explore the German programme →

How can I know the fees and timings?

Fees and batch timings may depend on the programme and class. Contact the academy directly for current details.

How do I enquire about admission?

Use the admission enquiry form on the homepage or contact TASVIA by phone or WhatsApp.

Is there a Parent Portal?

Yes. TASVIA has a Parent Portal for relevant updates such as attendance, tests, fees and homework. Access is provided to registered parents.
